2. If Q3 non-GAAP adjusted operating margin stays near or above the 3.0% level from Q2, it would confirm FirstFleet synergies are offsetting restructuring noise and support the EPS accretion story over the next year.
3. The Q2 GAAP operating margin collapse to 1.8% is already in the stock; the next two quarters are the real test — if GAAP margin climbs back above 2% and avoids a third straight quarter below that threshold, the bear case loses its main falsifier.
4. FirstFleet added scale in dedicated, which is higher-margin; as the combined dedicated revenue mix moves toward and stays above 45%, WERN’s earnings quality should improve through 2027 even if spot rates stay volatile.
5. A tight freight market and regulatory tightening are tailwinds for pricing and moat; if these conditions persist, WERN can pass through costs and expand margins over the next 12-18 months without needing a sharp spot-rate rebound.

7. Restructuring charges need to stay below $50 million in any single quarter; the November print and 2027 outlook will show whether the heavy cash outlays are behind the company, which matters for free cash flow and balance-sheet flexibility.
